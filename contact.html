FAQ

Common Questions

How long does deployment take? +

Standard industrial arm deployments typically take 6–12 weeks from order to first production. This includes site survey, mechanical installation, AI model configuration, safety testing, and operator training. AMR deployments can be faster — as little as 2 weeks for environments with standard infrastructure.

What's the ROI timeline? +

Our customers average an 18-month payback period, though we've seen manufacturing customers achieve ROI within 9 months in high-volume environments. We provide a free ROI modelling tool during the sales process so you can forecast based on your specific cost structure.

Do robots require internet connectivity? +

No. All our robots run full AI inference onboard using the NexusChip ASIC and operate completely autonomously without cloud connectivity. Internet connectivity enables fleet analytics, OTA updates, and federated learning improvements — but it's optional and can be air-gapped for secure facilities.

What support is included? +

All robots include 12 months of comprehensive support, including 24/7 remote monitoring, quarterly preventive maintenance visits, and unlimited software updates. Extended support plans are available. We maintain a global field service network with engineers in 28 countries.
