NexusAI is our vertically integrated AI platform — from custom silicon to cloud fleet management — built specifically for real-world robotics.

Core Capabilities

Six AI Pillars That Power Motion

👁

Computer Vision

Custom-trained vision models recognise objects, defects, and spatial environments at 120fps with sub-pixel accuracy. Works in low light, occlusion, and high-speed scenarios.

🧭

SLAM Navigation

Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping lets robots build and update a world model in real time — navigating dynamic environments without pre-mapped layouts.

🤖

Reinforcement Learning

Robots learn manipulation tasks through millions of simulated trials before deployment. Transfer learning adapts pre-trained skills to new tasks in hours, not months.

🌐

Fleet Intelligence

Federated learning across the entire deployed fleet. Every robot's experience improves all others — continuously, without centralising sensitive operational data.

⚡

Edge AI Inference

Custom NexusChip ASIC delivers 45 TOPS at 8W per robot — enabling full neural network inference onboard with 5ms end-to-end latency, no cloud dependency required.

🔐

Safety AI

Multi-model redundant safety architecture monitors for human proximity, unexpected objects, and robot state anomalies — triggering safe stops in under 50ms.

RoboSim Cloud

Train in Simulation, Deploy with Confidence

RoboSim is our physics-accurate cloud simulation environment where robots learn tasks through billions of virtual trials before touching real hardware. Domain randomisation teaches robots to handle real-world variation and unpredictability.

APIs allow integration with popular ML frameworks including PyTorch, JAX, and Stable-Baselines3. Simulation results transfer to physical robots with over 90% skill retention — no fine-tuning required.

  • 🔬
    Physics-Accurate Engine

    Rigid body, soft body, and fluid dynamics at 10,000x real-time speed

  • 🎲
    Domain Randomisation

    Automatically vary lighting, textures, mass, and friction during training

  • 📡
    Sim-to-Real Transfer

    90%+ skill retention from simulation to physical deployment
