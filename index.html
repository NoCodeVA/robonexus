The Process

How AI-Robotics Actually Works

01

Sense & Perceive

LiDAR, cameras, and tactile sensors feed real-time data into onboard AI models that build a complete picture of the environment.

02

Reason & Decide

Edge-deployed neural networks process sensor data in milliseconds, choosing optimal actions based on task context and safety parameters.

03

Act & Execute

Precision actuators carry out decisions with sub-millimetre accuracy — welding, picking, navigating, or assembling with zero drift.

04

Learn & Improve

Continuous federated learning lets the entire robot fleet share insights — every task performed makes the whole network smarter.
