Our Story

We Believe Robots and Humans Are Better Together

Founded in 2018, RoboNexus was built on a simple thesis: AI-powered robots should handle the work that puts humans at risk, while humans focus on what only humans can do.

Mission

Automating Danger, Amplifying Human Potential

Every year, millions of workers are injured performing repetitive, hazardous, or physically demanding tasks that robots can and should be doing. Our mission is to accelerate the deployment of safe, intelligent robots into every industry where human lives and wellbeing are at risk.

We don't build robots to replace people. We build robots so people no longer have to do the work that hurts them — freeing human talent for creativity, leadership, and care.

By 2030, we aim to prevent 1 million workplace injuries annually through robotic automation across our global customer base.

Values

What We Stand For

🛡

Safety First

No robot we build will ever compromise human safety. Every design decision starts with the question: how could this harm someone?

🔭

Long-Term Thinking

We build for decades, not quarters. Our robots are designed to be maintained, upgraded, and adapted as technology evolves.

🌍

Responsible Automation

We partner with customers on workforce transition planning, ensuring displaced workers are retrained for roles in the automated world.

🔬

Science-Driven

Every claim we make is backed by data. Every robot we ship has been tested under 10,000+ operating hours of simulation and real-world trials.

🤝

Open Collaboration

We publish research, open-source non-core software, and collaborate with universities to advance the entire robotics field.

♻️

Sustainability

All our robots use recyclable materials wherever possible. Our manufacturing facilities run on 100% renewable energy.

History

Our Journey So Far

2018

Founded in San Francisco

Three robotics PhD graduates from MIT and Stanford launch RoboNexus with $4M seed funding and a 10-person team.

2019

First Industrial Deployment

NX-Arm v1 deploys at AutoForge manufacturing facility in Detroit. 94% defect reduction in first quarter.

2020

Series A — $32M

Expansion into logistics with SwiftBot AMR. Partnership with 3 major e-commerce fulfilment networks.

2021

NexusAI Platform Launch

Proprietary AI stack released as unified platform across all robot lines. Fleet intelligence network goes live.

2022

Education Division

RoboNexus Education launched. First 500 school partnerships. EduBot Spark ships to 40 countries.

2023

Series C — $180M

Global expansion to 28 countries. 200+ employees. Agriculture and healthcare robot lines launched.

2024

NexusChip ASIC

Custom AI chip delivers 10× efficiency improvement. 500 robots deployed across 12 industries.

2025

Today & Beyond

1,200 employees across 6 offices. Targeting 1M robots deployed by 2028 and 1M injuries prevented by 2030.
